# Assessment Basis and Evidence Status

**October 6, 2026 | Fictional Northgate scenario | Documentation assessment**

This portfolio applies a structured assessment method to authored scenario facts. No actual clinic interviews, configuration exports, patient records, endpoint scans, access reviews or recovery tests were supplied. Statements about Northgate are assumptions for analysis, not observed conditions at a real organization.

## Evidence categories

| Category | Meaning | How it is used |
|---|---|---|
| Scenario assumption | Clinic size, systems, workflows, deficiencies and existing safeguards authored for this exercise | Supports hypothetical risk reasoning; cannot substantiate a real audit finding |
| External reference | Published regulation, framework or enforcement example cited in the documents | Supports criteria/context; does not prove clinic conformity or an enforcement outcome |
| Requested assurance | Implementation/test records listed in the management priorities and roadmap | Future evidence required to validate a control or close a gap |
| Projected risk | Analyst estimate of a possible post-treatment score | Conditional target; actual residual risk remains unassessed |

Use the [asset inventory](asset-inventory.md) and [threat–vulnerability matrix](threat-vulnerability-matrix.md) to trace assessment scope. The [register](risk-register.md) is the authoritative twelve-risk score record. The [management summary](management-priorities.md) groups decisions without introducing extra scores. The [roadmap](../05-remediation/remediation-roadmap.md) proposes actions and estimates.

## Interpretation rules

- A missing or outdated risk analysis does not establish that every other control is absent or ineffective.
- A signature approves a document; it does not prove the assessment is accurate or the treatment operates.
- Likelihood levels are ordinal judgment under stated assumptions, not probabilities, inevitability or evidence that an incident has occurred.
- Existing controls receive no effectiveness credit beyond what the scenario actually describes. Implementation assurance must be reviewed separately.
- Framework alignment is not certification. Regulatory applicability and current legal interpretation require appropriate review before real-world use.
- Cost estimates and target dates need validation by actual service owners. They are not procurement quotes, accepted SLAs or measured loss estimates.

Assessment preparation is distinct from regulatory-currency assurance. Historical references retain their original context; this documentation update does not represent a fresh legal review of every citation.
